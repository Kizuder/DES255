Emotion - Pressure

I chose to use pressure as my emotion of study as, from my perspective, pressure is a feeling that will almost always be a part of everyone in some way shape or form, be it studying for an exam, having to take care of something or someone for the first time, or an upcoming competition for a sport.

I want to investigate and work more on this emotion as I believe that despite being a negative emotion, it can also be a driving force for growth, in moderation. While it is good to work very hard towards a goal, as pressure can create diamonds, it can also be a person’s downfall, in the way that pressure is also capable of creating rubble.
